# Intro~Attack Flow

1. Problem: tool-calling agents mix trusted instructions and untrusted data.
2. Gap: prior work often stays at model-level injection, not channel measurement.
3. Threat model: attacker poisons one untrusted channel.
4. Attack: four channels, two payload styles, two tasks.
5. Finding: retrieved factual content works best; commands/notes are refused more often.
6. Boundary: this paper measures the gap; stronger document-style detection is next.
